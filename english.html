The Problem

Our neighborhood, the Bellamybuurt, is facing a growing problem caused by the feeding of ring-necked parakeets and, to some extent, pigeons. This issue arises because some residents continue to feed these birds despite clear prohibitions.

Key Issues:

  • Rapidly increasing bird populations, causing more nuisance.
  • Daily disturbance from screeching birds, especially early in the morning.
  • Food scraps left behind attract pests like rats and mice.
  • Unsafe situations around homes and balconies as birds become aggressive while searching for food.

What You Can Do

If you also experience nuisance caused by these birds, please take action:

  • Report the issue: Email us at vogeloverlastbellamybuurt@proton.me.
  • Contact Stadgenoot: If they are your landlord, report the nuisance here.
  • Contact Gemeente Amsterdam: Report the issue via MORA here.

Why Feeding Is Prohibited

Feeding birds is prohibited because:

  • It causes significant nuisance to residents.
  • It disrupts the natural balance in the city.
  • It leads to unnaturally high bird populations that become dependent on human food.

Working Together on a Solution

We hope that, as a community, we can address this problem together. Your voice matters! Let’s ensure our neighborhood remains a pleasant place to live.

If you have any questions or need more information, feel free to contact us at vogeloverlastbellamybuurt@proton.me
